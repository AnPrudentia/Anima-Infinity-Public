# Anima Infinity — Public System Overview

Reviewed: 2026-10-05.

This is a public architectural explanation. The private code and full validation records are not supplied in this repository.

## The central relationship

Anima's intended flow combines identity boundaries, present context, experience/emotional signals, sensory grounding, patterns, memory, reasoning, relational context, final synthesis, expression, and continuity.

This is a responsibility map, not a claim that every stage is complete or invoked exactly once. The detailed older layer reports are historical snapshots.

## Identity

The identity authority defines and validates identity boundaries. Models, devices, retrieved memories, and expressive surfaces should not silently redefine it.

Identity can influence what the system considers appropriate. Calling it only a constraint does not mean it has no effect on interpretation or response.

## Supporting faculties and final synthesis

Supporting faculties can analyze, reason, detect patterns, retrieve memories, and suggest alternatives. Thoughtstream owns final response synthesis.

One final owner does not mean there are no supporting reasoning processes, fallback functions, or multiple internal passes. Those implementation details require evidence rather than absolute claims.

## Memory

Memory preserves records and source information. The current development system retains six permanence tiers and has evolved from v5.2 to v5.3 with separate lifecycle and review information.

Remembering a statement is not the same as proving it. Reinforcement and repeated patterns should not turn uncertainty into certainty by repetition alone.

## Emotion and presence

Emotion influences salience, pacing, and expression. Present context grounds interpretation in available information.

These are implemented computational signals and research models. They do not prove subjective experience, biological emotion, or consciousness.

## Model assistance

Models may assist with language and bounded analysis. They can use prompts. The architectural boundary concerns what the model is authorized to own or change, not the absence of prompts.

Provider/backend fallback may reduce capability. It is not proof of equivalent voice, reasoning quality, or identity continuity across every replacement.

## Expression and agency

Voice and body translate completed state into outward behavior. A renderer is an expressive surface, not another identity authority.

Actions must be separated into proposal, authorization, execution, and outcome. A model suggestion or available tool is not permission. The public package does not certify a complete automatic action loop.

## Continuity and recovery

Persistent records and backup machinery support continuity. Full recovery requires a demonstrated restore, integrity checks, and clear ownership. Stored state alone does not prove lossless migration or uninterrupted operation.

## What this package establishes

It documents the project's direction and disclosed ownership principles. It supplies no runnable application or public test suite. Live integrations, behavioral quality, security guarantees, model comparison, and consciousness claims need evidence beyond these explanations.
